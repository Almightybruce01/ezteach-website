Features

Everything your school needs

EZTeach delivers the same features across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Use the app on any device; use this site to learn more, subscribe, and download.

📊

Grades & Assignments

Points-based entry, weighted categories, auto-calculated overall grades. Override support for teachers and schools.

✓

Attendance & Analytics

Daily tracking, analytics, dashboards, and trends. Present, tardy, absent, excused — school-wide insights.

👪

Parent Portal

Parents link via student code and see their child's grades, homework, and attendance. Secure and private.

🔄

Substitute Management

Request subs, approve jobs, assign coverage. Sub plans, availability, and sub rankings in the app.

🤖

AI Lesson Plans

Generate intelligent, standards-aligned lesson plans with pictures, videos, and self-learning capability. Aligned to CCSS, NGSS, TEKS, and more.

🧠

AI Study Plans for Parents

Personalized study plans with milestones, resources, and in-app suggestions. Parents and students track mastery and fill knowledge gaps.

🎮

EZLearning Games & Leaderboards

20+ educational games — math, spelling, reading, patterns, and more. Student-name leaderboards with school and grade.

📚

Free Books & Reading Together

Picture books, chapter books, and thousands of classics. Multiplayer “Reading Together” sessions for collaborative reading.

📕

School Library Management

Full catalog with check-in/out, day tracking, digital books from photos, ISBN/barcode support, and overdue alerts. Built for library teachers.

🎬

Educational Movies

120+ curated public domain films — short and feature-length. Nature docs, classic cinema, science films, and more. Safe for classrooms.

📝

Homework & Photo Submissions

Assign homework by class with due dates. Students and parents submit photos and files directly in the app.

🎥

Video Meetings

Schedule meetings with codes, join by link or code. In-app camera UI for teachers, parents, staff, and admins.

💬

Messaging System

Direct and group messaging for all staff and parents. Threaded conversations with timestamps and read receipts.

📐

Dynamic Standards System

CCSS, NGSS, TEKS, SHAPE America, CSTA, and more. District and school overrides. Searchable Standards Explorer with AI integration.

🗣

AAC Talker Board

Augmentative communication board with categories, pictures, sounds, and customizable cards. Available for students and teachers.

🚌

Bus Tracking & Lunch Menu

Real-time bus locations for parents. Weekly lunch menus and daily specials managed by schools.

🔔

Bell Schedules & Calendar

Bell schedule management, school-wide calendar events, and announcements from principals and teachers.

⚠

Emergency Alerts & Behavior

School-wide emergency alerts, behavior tracking, student write-ups, and discipline management.

🎨

Electives Hub

Art, music, dance, band, and P.E. Dedicated section for elective classes with grade-level support.

🏢

District Management

Districts manage multiple schools, set standards overrides, view analytics, and subscribe with per-student pricing. Optional district codes for schools.
